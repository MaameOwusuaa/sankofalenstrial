History

Osu Castle

📍 Osu, Accra, Ghana

Osu Castle, also known as Christiansborg Castle, is a historic coastal fortress in Accra. Its history reflects centuries of European trading activity and Ghana's changing political history.

A Brief History

The site has a long history involving European powers and local communities. The Danes developed the fort into Christiansborg, and the site later became an important seat of government in Ghana.

Today, Osu Castle is an important symbol of Ghana's political and colonial history and forms part of Ghana's UNESCO-listed Forts and Castles heritage property.

History

Cape Coast Castle

📍 Cape Coast, Central Region

Cape Coast Castle is one of Ghana's most significant historic landmarks. The castle played an important role in European trade and the transatlantic slave trade.

A Brief History

Cape Coast Castle developed within the network of European forts and castles established along Ghana's coast from the late 15th century onward.

It later became one of the major sites associated with the transatlantic slave trade. Today, the castle serves as a powerful historical and educational site and forms part of Ghana's UNESCO World Heritage property.

Nature

Kakum National Park

📍 Central Region, Ghana

Kakum National Park is a tropical rainforest reserve known for its famous canopy walkway, rich biodiversity, birdlife, and forest experience.

A Brief History

Kakum covers approximately 375 square kilometres of tropical rainforest in Ghana's Central Region. The area was first reserved in 1931 and was later declared a national park in 1992.

Its most famous attraction is the canopy walkway, a series of suspension bridges that allow visitors to experience the rainforest from above the forest floor.
